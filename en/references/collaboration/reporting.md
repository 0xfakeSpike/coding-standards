# Reporting

### Lead with the conclusion [Required]

Give the result or judgment in the first sentence, then expand. Keep it concise and organized. Do not narrate the steps one by one.

**Source:** Team

### Translate terms into practical meaning [Required]

Do not just drop a term. Explain what it means for the user.

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| The migration has not been run from scratch on an empty database | The migration scripts are not verified yet: deploying to a brand-new database may fail to create tables |

**Source:** Team

For how to write up done, not done, and pending decisions, see "Report verification results truthfully" in [change-workflow.md](change-workflow.md).

### Give a recommendation when a decision is needed [Recommended]

List the trade-offs, then give a recommended option and the reason. Do not just list options.
