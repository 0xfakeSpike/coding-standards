## Usage limits

- Sources whose license is "Not found" or "Not verified", and sources that forbid derivatives or commercial use or reserve all rights, such as Google SRE, Redis, Elasticsearch, and microservices.io, contribute ideas only. Do not copy their text or structure.
- When adding a source, verify the original text, version, and license before adding it to this table.
- When a public source conflicts with a team rule, follow the team rule. For example, Go convention requires doc comments on all exported identifiers, while the team rule does not enforce this.
